# Presentation and demo acceptance checklist

Require a hashed `presentation_checklist.json` and `demo_manifest.json` owned by
the orchestrator. Confirm each item resolves to a committed or artifact-backed
path and SHA-256.

- Scientist-confirmed Stage 0 association, provenance, and registration mode.
- Stage 1 design-only deletion method and 18/93/186 count gates.
- Selected production registration mode stated without overstating autonomy.
- Challenge and autonomous branches explained; autonomous CT-only freeze shown.
- Exact per-scan Otsu provenance and diagnostics.
- Independent local-node overlay and ambiguity/support evidence.
- Coarse padded-ROI gate distinguished from the stricter metrology gate.
- Requested analysis scope and authorized/unauthorized outputs shown; ROI-only
  evidence is never presented as direct dimensional metrology.
- Stage 1 orientation source disclosed as unambiguous geometry, verified
  declaration, or honest manual review, including transform evidence hashes.
- Stage 3 metrics/corridor calibration and worst-strut evidence views.
- Stage 4 fixed precedence, separate bent attribute, and verifier sign-off.
- Stage 5 one-shot protocol, strict/lenient recall with Wilson interval, and
  confusion matrix; no recall optimization loop or unsupported precision.
- Blind findings kept separate from the attribution appendix.
- Candidate unintentional-defect triage and human spot-check list.
- Spatial statistics figures and graph-based 3D defect render.
- Recompute-free NDE report with a passing number crosscheck.
- Missing/unimplemented agents or tools disclosed as structured halts, never
  represented by fabricated demo output.
